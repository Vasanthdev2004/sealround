# Lantern pilot artwork

The built-in image generation tool produced four demo panels. Mira and Sora are fictional artist personas with separate local sandbox accounts. The final WebP bytes are pinned by SHA-256 in the [Lantern manifest](../../public/chapters/lantern.json).

## Light the bridge

[lantern-1-a.webp](../../public/chapters/lantern-1-a.webp)

A single 16:9 landscape comic panel for an illustrated fable about a tiny pale-grey moth carrying a round amber paper lantern through a nocturnal seaside town. Story moment, the moth lights a crooked footbridge over dark tidewater; light runs along the rail, far houses remain dark. Composition with lantern near lower center, wood grain, sea mist, spare skyline. Artist A style: hand-printed risograph, ink stipple, limited ultramarine, ivory and warm amber, bold cuts, textured paper. Carefully composed indie graphic novel. Full bleed. No words, letters, frames, boxes, text or signature.

## Wake the lighthouse

[lantern-1-b.webp](../../public/chapters/lantern-1-b.webp)
